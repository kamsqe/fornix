Fourteen issues so far. Each one ships as a physical broadsheet and a parallel
web edition; the two share nothing but the typography. Online: long-form essays
in a single column, set to 88ch, with footnotes in the margin on desktops and
inline on phones. The phone reading experience is the canonical one.
